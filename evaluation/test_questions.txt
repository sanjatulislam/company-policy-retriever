2. Cross-document synthesis (answer spans 2+ docs)

Tests whether retrieval pulls chunks from multiple sources and generation combines them coherently — hardest and most useful tier.

 - How does SCC's Privacy by Design approach relate to its requirements for remote working with RED-level information? 
 - How does the Information Security Policy's incident reporting process relate to the specific incident-reporting steps for lost devices in the Acceptable Use Policy?
 - How do the RED/AMBER/GREEN classification definitions in the Data Protection Policy compare to those in the Acceptable Use Policy — are they consistent?
 - Which SCC role is responsible for both data protection compliance and reviewing DPIAs, and how does this connect to the AI product approval process?
 - How does the Information Security Policy's risk assessment process (DPIA) relate to the DPIA requirement mentioned for information sharing in the Data Protection Policy?


Expected: answer draws from multiple chunks, a good system should not say 'not found' if content exists split across documents.

3. Chunk-boundary stress tests

Given chunk_size=850/overlap=150, some answers may be split mid-list or mid-clause. These test whether retrieval + reassembly still produces a complete answer.

 - What are the required actions when an employee loses a laptop containing RED-classified personal data?
 - List all the steps involved in the WhatsApp de-registration and data transfer process.
 - What is the full list of content considered 'inappropriate' for staff to access or store on SCC IT facilities?
 - What are all the responsibilities of a Strategic Information Agent (SIA) across the policies?
 - Summarize every step in SCC's process for handling a security incident caused by staff misuse.

Expected: complete list preserved, nothing silently truncated mid-bullet.
